Who operates Nuts

Nuts is operated by DUCHENE SARL. For privacy questions or requests, contact thib.duchene@gmail.com. This policy describes the Nuts iOS app. Independent Nostr relays, remote signers, Cashu mints, hosting services and merchants have their own policies.

Information you provide

A Nostr public key identifies your account. Profile information, posts, follows, reactions, public bookmarks and public reports are distributed to Nostr relays. Other people and services may copy this information. Signing keys imported into the app are stored locally in the iOS Keychain. If you choose a remote signer, that service handles signing requests. Do not send private keys or wallet recovery phrases to support.

Messages and uploaded media

Direct messages are encrypted before relay delivery; routing identifiers and timing can remain visible to relays. Selected photo and video files are uploaded to your configured media host and their links may be public. Do not assume a media URL is private because you send it in an encrypted message. The app requests camera or photo access when needed for the feature you choose.

Notifications and network requests

If notifications are enabled, the app sends a push token, app/platform information and relay selection to the Nuts notification service using authentication linked to your public key. Apple delivers notifications. Requests to relays, mints, hosting services, link-preview resources, GIF search and other connected services may expose your IP address and requested content to those services.

Wallet and community purchases

Wallet operations send the information needed for payment to your chosen mint, Lightning service or community checkout provider. Wallet recovery data and ecash proofs are stored locally; the app supports encrypted Nostr wallet backups. Merchants and payment services process purchase information under their own terms and policies.

Why information is processed

Information is processed to provide the features you request, operate and protect the service, deliver notifications, handle support and abuse reports, and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on the processing and applicable law, the basis may be performance of our agreement with you, legitimate interests in operating a safe service, consent where required, or a legal obligation. You may disable optional permissions in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and caches support app operation. Delete account in Profile requests a deleted Nostr profile and removes the local identity and wallet data. Move any wallet balance before deleting. The app requires acknowledgment from the push service before deleting local account data when a push token exists. Independent services may retain copies of public events and media. Contact us to request deletion of information under DUCHENE SARL’s control. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or resolving disputes.

Your choices and rights

You can choose relays, mints and a signer, manage iOS permissions, block profiles, and request account deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. Email thib.duchene@gmail.com. We may ask you to demonstrate control of your public identity, but will not ask for your private key or recovery phrase.

International services and changes

Nostr is a global network and connected providers may process information in other countries. Check the policies of services you choose. Updates to this policy will be published here with an updated date; material changes will be communicated where required.
