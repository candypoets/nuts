Contact DUCHENE SARL

For support, abuse reports and privacy requests, email thib.duchene@gmail.com. Include your app version, device/iOS version, and the steps needed to reproduce the problem. Never include private keys, wallet recovery phrases or spendable ecash tokens.

Report abuse

Use Report in a post’s three-dot menu or on a profile. You can also email the public event/profile identifier and a description to thib.duchene@gmail.com. Nostr reports sent from the app are public. Block the profile to hide it from supported app surfaces. Contact emergency services directly if someone is in immediate danger.

Payments and recovery

Check wallet activity and pending-payment status before retrying. For mint redemption or merchant fulfillment, include the service name and non-secret transaction reference. Nuts support cannot recreate a lost key or reverse an independent payment.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Delete account. Transfer your wallet balance and save recovery information first. Public content may remain on independent services. For data held by DUCHENE SARL, contact the email above.
